The original tuned config reproduces the founding regression of this whole investigation: strongly positive at low concurrency, crossing zero around c=48, down to -4.2% at c=128 -- real serving traffic hits the same GROUP_SIZE_M=1 cost that 19's L2-flush isolated benchmark now shows directly at the kernel level. l2-flush never crosses zero anywhere in the sweep, from +30.5% at c=1 down to +3.8% at c=128 -- the same one-parameter-family fix, corrected, turns the entire curve positive. (The two lines are computed against different but methodologically-identical default sweeps, run on the same hardware/image with the same benchmark parameters -- not the same run, so small absolute differences at any one point could reflect ordinary sweep-to-sweep noise rather than a real difference; the qualitative story -- regression vs. no regression -- is not sensitive to that.)
