All three fixes hold up in the exact measurement that found the regression — including the maximally narrow one. At the real, top-of-range M actually reached in these captures (the "M 1024–2048" bucket, colloquially "M≈2048" in 05's original callout), gate_up_proj's weighted-mean duration: default 1111.0µs → tuned 1961.0µs (+76.5%, the blowout) → singlefix 1057.3µs (−4.8% vs. default, −46.1% vs. tuned) → groupfix 1038.3µs (−6.5% vs. default) → cache-aware-tuned 1035.0µs (−6.8% vs. default, −47.2% vs. tuned). singlefix changes exactly one value out of all 5 shapes' entire config — leaving gate_up_proj's other five large-M anchors (512/1024/1536/3072/4096) at the same GROUP_SIZE_M=1 as plain tuned — and still recovers most of the fix: ~74% of groupfix's improvement over default (53.8µs of 72.7µs), from a change touching one line in one config file. The remaining gap to groupfix/cache-aware-tuned is consistent with those other large-M anchors contributing a real, smaller additional effect, but the specific M-band that showed the blowout is overwhelmingly fixed by fixing that M-band alone.
