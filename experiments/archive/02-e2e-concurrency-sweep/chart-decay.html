Output tok/s delta (tuned vs. default) vs. concurrency

Same client traffic profile (--request-rate inf) at 10 concurrency levels, 16x-scaled sample counts, --max-num-seqs=128 (raised from 64 to test past the old scheduler ceiling). A smooth, monotonic decay -- not a step change -- consistent with a shrinking share of per-step time (GEMM is ~12% of a decode step at low load, ~3.5% at high load), not with suddenly hitting a specific weak batch size. Confirmed directly: even after raising the ceiling let real traffic reach M=160 (a flagged weak point), the curve kept decaying at the same rate rather than dropping further -- see the traffic-vs-speedup plot.

Shaded band marks the approximate noise floor observed across repeated e2e runs elsewhere in this investigation (a few percent) -- once the curve enters it, the sign of the last point or two is not a reliable indicator of a real effect.
