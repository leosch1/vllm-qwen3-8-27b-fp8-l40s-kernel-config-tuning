GEMM launch durations, split by model layer (weight shape) — 500 prompts

Every real captured _w8a8_triton_block_scaled_mm launch (from the original 500-prompt, c=64 captures — pass1-default-concurrency64-v2 / pass2-tuned-concurrency64-v2) reverse-mapped from its launch grid/block dimensions back to which of the model's 5 W8A8 weight matrices it belongs to — each a distinct FFN/MoE layer type. N=17408, N=8192, and N=7168 are each uniquely identifiable at any batch size. N=5120 covers two weight matrices (K=3072 and K=8704) that share the same N — default's config formula can never tell them apart (it doesn't depend on N/K at all), and tuned only can ~61% of the time, so both are merged into one N=5120 (combined) bucket here for a fair apples-to-apples comparison. ~3.5% of launches per config landed on a genuinely ambiguous grid signature and are excluded from every bucket below.

Per-layer proportions are nearly identical between configs (e.g. N=5120: 47.81% of default's launches vs. 48.31% of tuned's) — direct confirmation that both captures really are doing comparable logical work, the concern that originally motivated this breakdown.
N=17408 is the outlier. Every other layer gets faster under tuning (+5.4% to +23.4%) — but N=17408, the exact shape this whole GPU-metrics investigation has been chasing (it's where the ≥2000µs slow cluster lives), gets 27.4% slower on average — an even larger regression than the 150-prompt version of this same breakdown showed (21.6%). This is the layer actually responsible for tuned's c=64 regression; the other three layers are pure wins that the N=17408 regression outweighs in aggregate.
