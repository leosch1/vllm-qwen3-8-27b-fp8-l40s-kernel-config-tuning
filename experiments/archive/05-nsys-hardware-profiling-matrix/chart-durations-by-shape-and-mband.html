The multiple peaks in the plain duration histogram are literally different GEMM shapes, not noise — and they're not even one duration per shape: gate_up_proj, M ≤128 sits around 130–140µs, while gate_up_proj, M 2048–4096 reaches 1183µs under default and 2133µs under tuned — that largest-M gate_up_proj entry is exactly the tail chart.html's mean/median-gap analysis was chasing. Every other shape only ever populates its 2–3 smallest-M legend entries in either config — they see the same range of concurrent batch sizes as gate_up_proj, but only gate_up_proj's largest-M entries turn into disproportionately long launches, confirming 08's finding that the regression is specific to that one weight matrix at large M.
