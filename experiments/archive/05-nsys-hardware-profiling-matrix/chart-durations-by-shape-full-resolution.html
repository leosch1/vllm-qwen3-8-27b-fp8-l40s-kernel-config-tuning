The multiple peaks in the plain duration histogram are literally different GEMM shapes, not noise — and now every one of gate_up_proj's ~10 distinct real M-values shows up as its own colored segment, tracing a near-continuous light-to-dark progression from its smallest M (~130–140µs) up to its single largest-M entry: 1183µs under default, 2133µs under tuned — that one specific entry is exactly the tail chart.html's mean/median-gap analysis was chasing. Every other shape only ever populates its 2–5 smallest-M entries in either config — they see the same range of concurrent batch sizes as gate_up_proj, but only gate_up_proj's largest-M entries turn into disproportionately long launches, confirming 08's finding that the regression is specific to that one weight matrix at large M.
