Same 175-point grid as this experiment's other charts, comparing 22's maximally-narrow config against the original, unpatched tuned config it's a one-value edit of — but unlike cache-aware/groupfix's vs.-tuned charts (which chained through two separately-run vs.-default passes), this one benchmarks tuned_config(M) and singlefix_config(M) back-to-back in one script execution, same counterbalanced cycling design as every other chart here.
