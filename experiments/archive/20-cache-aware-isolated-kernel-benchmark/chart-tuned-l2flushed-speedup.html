Isolated kernel speedup, original tuned config vs. default, under L2-flush pressure

Same 175-point grid as this folder's cache-cycling chart, this time timed under 24's direct L2 flush before every timed launch instead of 17's 64-copy cycling — for the plain, unpatched original tuned config (this repo's tuned-configs/, GROUP_SIZE_M=1 at every large-M anchor on gate_up_proj).

This is the clearest demonstration of the GROUP_SIZE_M=1 regression this project has produced. Under always-warm (01) and cache-cycling (this folder's other chart) pressure, gate_up_proj's large-M points stayed mildly positive or near zero. Under a full L2 flush before every single launch — the coldest cache condition possible — gate_up_proj swings sharply negative exactly where GROUP_SIZE_M=1 was picked: -47.5% at M=512, -34.4% at M=768, -23.7% at M=1024, -19.1% at M=1280, -11.4% at M=1536, -9.1% at M=1792, -6.0% at M=2048, only recovering to positive by M=2560+. No other shape shows anything close to this pattern. (One isolated outlier, M=40 held-out at -64.6%, doesn't fit this large-M pattern and is likely a single pathological config/tile-size interaction under the most extreme flush condition -- flagged, not folded into the story above.) 13/175 points negative overall.
