Isolated kernel speedup, original tuned config vs. default, under cache-cycling pressure — averaged across shapes

Same data as the 5-shape version (same 175-point grid, same cache-cycling methodology, plain unpatched original tuned config), collapsed to a single line: the unweighted mean speedup across all 5 shapes at each M, instead of 5 separate per-shape lines.

Averaging smooths out the shape-to-shape noise that dominates the 5-line version at small M (single points there swing as wide as -20.5% to +26.5%) and makes the underlying trend easy to read directly: mean speedup dips slightly negative only briefly around M=160 (-3.8%), and otherwise sits solidly positive across the whole grid, rising from roughly +7-9% in the low-to-mid M range to +10-11% at the largest M anchors (3072-4096). No single-shape point should be read off this line, though — averaging can hide a real per-shape effect (like gate_up_proj's own large-M behavior) inside a mean dominated by the other 4 shapes.
